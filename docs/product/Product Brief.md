# PickOne Product Brief

## Product

PickOne is an iOS app that helps people decide what movie to watch faster.

## Problem

Users often spend too much time browsing and comparing titles before making a decision. The product reduces that decision fatigue with focused discovery and recommendation flows.

## Audience

- people who want a faster movie-picking experience
- users who maintain a lightweight watchlist
- users who want conversational help when they do not know what to watch

## Core Value Proposition

- discover strong movie options quickly
- save movies for later without friction
- search reliably across the catalog
- ask for recommendation help in natural language

## Current Product Surface

- top rated discovery feed
- movie detail
- similar movies
- credits
- watchlist
- search
- search history

## Product Principles

- optimize for faster decisions, not infinite browsing
- keep the interface simple and legible
- prefer a small number of strong actions over feature overload
- treat AI as a product aid, not as decoration

## Non-Goals for the Current Stage

- social features
- reviews and ratings from users
- drag and drop watchlist ordering
- heavy personalization infrastructure
- offline-first AI experiences
